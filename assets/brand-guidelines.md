# SZARA logo system

The master wordmark is traced directly from the Facebook Field Notes reference image provided by the owner, with black lettering and cobalt square (#0838F1). Use szara-wordmark.svg on light backgrounds and szara-wordmark-white.svg on dark backgrounds. Both share the same outlined geometry. Use szara-brand-square.svg for square logo placements and favicon.svg for browser icons. Do not retype, stretch, substitute or recolor the mark. The source is a compressed social graphic, not the original editable design.

## Website palette

- Master logo: #080808 and #0838F1. White variant on dark surfaces.
- Midnight navy #102438; interface accent #306B85; hover #1B556B; ink #15394A; muted #526B76; line #D9E4E7; surface #F5F9F9; pale blue #B9E2EF; white #FFFFFF.
- Cobalt is reserved for the logo square and brand signatures; interface buttons use the Houston blue. Preserve skyline photography, astronomical colors and legitimate status colors.
- All content pages load brand-system.css and the mobile navigation styles. Social preview and raster icons use the same vector source or compact S monogram. Do not replace the logo with typed lettering.
