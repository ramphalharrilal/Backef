Care and companion service websites

A clear website for
the people who need you.

When families explore care and companion services, they need to understand what's offered, where it's available and how to ask questions. SZARA builds straightforward websites for service providers who want those answers to be easy to find.

Discuss a website

Help visitors understand the services

Someone looking for support for a family member may be unfamiliar with the difference between residential accommodation, companionship, sitter services and skilled medical care. A good website should explain what the business actually provides, rather than leaving families to guess.

We start by making the service descriptions specific: who the service is for, which areas are covered, how an enquiry works, and what a visitor should ask about before making arrangements.

The important details deserve their own space

Depending on the provider, a website might need:

  • Separate explanations for each service, with clear eligibility or suitability boundaries.
  • Service areas and contact methods that are easy to find on a phone.
  • A simple enquiry form that asks only for the information needed to reply.
  • Information about the operator, hours, and how arrangements are confirmed.
  • Appropriate photographs, with permission to use them.

We don't invent credentials, licenses, testimonials or health outcomes. Any regulated claims must be reviewed and approved by the business. Website enquiries should not be treated as emergency or clinical assessment channels.

Experience with Tru Luv Home & Sitters

Harry built the Tru Luv Home & Sitters website, which presents residential boarding for independent adults separately from in-home companion and sitter services. Visitors can read about the available services and find a direct way to contact the operator.

It's one example of why careful wording matters. A site can feel warm and welcoming without implying that every kind of care is available. You can also see the project in SZARA's portfolio.
