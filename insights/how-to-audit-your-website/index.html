How to Audit Your Website and Know What Actually Needs Fixing

By SZARA · Design and technology resources

Learn how to assess website performance, usability, SEO and customer experience, and how SZARA Insight turns findings into practical recommendations.

Quick answer

Start with free testing and a real customer journey, then prioritize verified issues by impact. A professional audit can explain causes, document findings and recommend targeted improvements before you invest in a rebuild.

Explore website assessments

Before spending money on a website redesign, determine whether your current website needs a full rebuild or a few targeted improvements. A useful audit looks beyond a single automated score and considers the needs of your business and its customers.

Start with free tools and a real customer journey

Use Google PageSpeed Insights to compare mobile and desktop pages. Review search discovery in Google Search Console if you manage the website. Then visit your site as a first-time customer. Is the service clear? Can you find contact details? Do menus, links, forms, booking and payment options work? Are prices and policies current? Are there genuine reviews or examples of work?

Automated tools identify many technical issues, but cannot understand the purpose of every design decision or whether the site is serving your business. Human testing complements automation.

A practical example from our own website

At SZARA, we optimized our own Houston skyline website without removing its visual identity or interactive design. In documented laboratory tests, the mobile Lighthouse performance score improved from 71 to 81 and LCP decreased from 9.97 seconds to 4.95 seconds. Desktop performance reached 96.

We used more efficient image formats, responsive image delivery, stylesheet optimization and improved loading of visual assets. The measured improvement did not require replacing the design. These were controlled lab results, not real-world visitor measurements; performance varies by device, connection and test run. Mobile LCP still had room for improvement.

What SZARA Insight can assess

  • Performance: loading delays, oversized assets and inefficient scripts.
  • Mobile and desktop usability: responsive layout, navigation, readability and interactions.
  • Technical SEO: indexing, metadata, page structure, broken links and discovery barriers.
  • Accessibility: selected contrast, keyboard, label and readability checks; not a certification of compliance.
  • Customer experience: customer journeys, enquiry and booking flows, and important calls to action.
  • Content and credibility: service clarity, visible reputation signals and business information.
  • Reliability and security: observable concerns within the agreed authorized scope; not an unrestricted penetration test.
