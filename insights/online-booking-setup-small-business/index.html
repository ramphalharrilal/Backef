How to Set Up Online Booking for a Small Business

Learn how to choose a booking workflow, collect the right customer details and test confirmations before launch.

Online booking should make it easier for customers to reach you and easier for your team to manage requests. Start by deciding whether customers need a confirmed appointment or just a request for a quote. Those two workflows have different requirements.

Choose the simplest useful workflow

A contractor may need a service request with a ZIP code and optional photograph. A salon might need a calendar showing real availability. A consultant might need a short intake form followed by a call. Write down the information you actually need and avoid collecting unnecessary personal details.

Connect the tools you already use

Check whether your website platform supports the booking service, who receives notifications, and how cancellations or schedule changes work. If deposits are needed, confirm payment terms and refund handling separately. Keep third-party subscriptions and processing fees in mind.

Test before customers use it

Complete a test request on a phone and desktop. Check that the right team member receives it, that the confirmation is clear and that unavailable times cannot be booked accidentally. Use dummy details and avoid real customer data.
