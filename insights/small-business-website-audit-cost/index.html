How Much Does a Website Audit Cost for a Small Business?

Compare website audit scope, page counts and SZARA Insight assessment prices in USD. Learn what to look for before purchasing.

A website audit is a structured review of how well a site serves its visitors. It can reveal problems with navigation, mobile usability, page performance and the path from a visitor's first click to an enquiry. An assessment is not the same as a redesign or a promise to increase sales.

What affects the price?

The number of pages, the complexity of the platform and the depth of review all matter. A five-page brochure website is different from an online store, a membership website or a large archive. Ask whether the assessment covers individual pages or only a sample, and whether it includes prioritized recommendations.

SZARA Insight pricing

  • Essential: US$299 one time. Assessment covering up to five pages.
  • Professional: US$799 one time. Assessment covering up to 25 pages.
  • Enterprise: from US$2,499 by quotation. Custom assessment covering up to 100 pages, subject to approved scope.

These are SZARA's advertised prices, not an industry-wide average. Confirm the scope, deliverables, access requirements and payment terms before purchasing. Only submit a site you own or are authorized to have assessed.

What should you do with the findings?

Separate problems that prevent customers from completing essential tasks from cosmetic improvements. Fix critical contact and navigation issues first, then verify the changes with a real customer journey. Remediation is a separate service unless expressly included.
