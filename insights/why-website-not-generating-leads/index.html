Why Your Small Business Website Is Not Generating Leads

Identify common barriers to website enquiries, from unclear service information to weak calls to action and broken contact forms.

A website can receive visits without generating enquiries. The cause might be the wrong audience, unclear information, a slow or confusing mobile experience, or a contact method that does not work. Avoid assuming that a redesign or more advertising is the answer.

Start with the visitor's intent

Someone searching for a local repair service needs different information from someone comparing software or professional services. Make the service, location or service area, and next step easy to understand. Explain what a customer can expect after contacting you.

Remove unnecessary friction

Open the site on a phone. Can you read the text without zooming, find a phone number or enquiry button, and complete the form without excessive questions? Use clear labels, avoid intrusive overlays and provide alternative contact methods where appropriate.

Check that enquiries arrive

Submit a test enquiry and confirm that it reaches the correct inbox. A success message alone does not prove delivery. Check booking links, phone links and notifications. See our contact form troubleshooting guide.

Measure before changing everything

Use your analytics and Search Console, where available, to understand which pages attract visitors and what search queries bring them in. Track meaningful enquiries rather than assuming every click is a sale. Make targeted changes, then measure again.
