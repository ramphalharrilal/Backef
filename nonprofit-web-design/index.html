Nonprofit web design

Make your mission
easy to understand.

A visitor should be able to understand who you help, what you do and how to take the next step. We design nonprofit and community websites around those questions.

Tell us about your project ↗

Different visitors need different answers

Someone seeking help may need program information and contact details. A volunteer may need to understand how to get involved. A supporter may want to see the mission and the people behind it. We agree these audiences and priorities before deciding the page list.

The result should be a useful structure, not a homepage trying to say everything at once. Clear page names, descriptive headings and visible contact routes help visitors find their way on a phone or desktop.

What we can help organize

  • Mission, organization background and founder information.
  • Programs, services and practical next steps for the people you serve.
  • Approved stories, videos, photographs and client feedback.
  • Contact information and enquiry forms.
  • Public resources, documents and updates where agreed.

Your organization supplies and approves the factual content. Donation tools, event registration, member areas, sensitive intake processes and database integrations need separate assessment and scope. A basic contact form is not a suitable place to collect sensitive case information.

Real work for a US nonprofit

Gaining Ground Youth Services is a Houston based nonprofit represented in Harry’s portfolio. The work organized its mission, services, testimonials, videos and Dale Ray’s story into a public website. Read the case study to see the contribution and screenshot.

For another type of community information, Kenswick Meadows Community Association demonstrates resident resources, forms, documents and ongoing management. These are distinct projects with different audiences and responsibilities.

Clear scope for limited time and resources

Tell us the people you need to reach, the information they need and who will approve the work. We agree the pages, supplied content, third party tools and review process in a written quote. That helps a small staff or volunteer team know what they need to provide and when.

US quotes are in USD. We work remotely and agree communication times around your US time zone; Caribbean and other international organizations are also welcome. Your project contact is Harry.

Plan for the work after launch

Programs, contact details and resources can change. Agree who will keep them current and whether ongoing website maintenance and support is appropriate. Support scope and response expectations belong in the proposal, alongside any platform fees.

A focused nonprofit site may fit the five page website and Care package; a larger program or resource website needs a custom quote. Read the broader website design process or tell us about your organization below.
