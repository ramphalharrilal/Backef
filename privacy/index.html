Your enquiry

How your details
reach SZARA.

When you contact SZARA, SZARA receives the details you provide so he can discuss your request and prepare a proposal.

What the form collects

Your name, email address and message. The enquiry also includes the page it was sent from and simple campaign labels in the page link, when present. These help us understand where enquiries come from. This source tracking does not use cookies, local storage or a visitor identifier. Please do not include passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information.

Optional website analytics

Google Analytics loads only if you choose Accept analytics. Rejecting it leaves the website and enquiry form working normally. Use Privacy choices at the bottom of any page to change your decision.

After acceptance, Google receives page visits, basic browser and device information and a signal when you return from an enquiry submission. Form answers, names and email addresses are not sent to Analytics. Page query strings and fragments are removed from the page address sent by our tracking code. Automatic form tracking and advertising features are disabled.

Your choice is stored in this browser for up to 180 days. If accepted, Analytics uses cookies to distinguish visits, configured to expire after 180 days. After Formspree accepts the submission, a temporary marker in this tab records the form page and acceptance time so an ordinary thank you page visit is not counted as an enquiry return. It contains no form answers, expires for counting purposes after 30 minutes and is removed when used. This signal does not confirm email delivery or a qualified lead.

Rejecting analytics later disables it and removes its cookies on this site. This does not erase data already processed by Google. Blocking storage or analytics, declining consent or closing the tab can prevent measurement. See Google’s privacy policy.

Where the information goes

The form is submitted to Formspree, which processes and stores submissions in its dashboard and applies spam protection. It sends the enquiry by email to hello@withszara.com. Cloudflare Email Routing forwards that email to the owner's Gmail inbox. These services process information under their own policies.

Formspree privacy policy · Cloudflare privacy policy · Google privacy policy

How to ask about your details

Email hello@withszara.com to ask about your enquiry information, request a correction or request deletion. You can also contact SZARA directly by email instead of using the form.
