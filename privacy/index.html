Your enquiry

How your details
reach SZARA.

When you contact SZARA, Ramphal Harrilal receives the details you provide so he can discuss your request and prepare a proposal.

What the form collects

Your name, email address, optional business name, selected service, message and, if you choose to answer, how you found us. The enquiry also includes the page it was sent from and simple campaign labels in the page link, when present. These help us understand where enquiries come from. This source tracking does not use cookies, local storage or a visitor identifier. Please do not include passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information.

Where the information goes

The form is submitted to FormSubmit, which processes the submission and provides a spam check. It sends the enquiry by email to hello@withszara.com. Cloudflare Email Routing forwards that email to the owner's Gmail inbox. These services process information under their own policies.

FormSubmit privacy policy · Cloudflare privacy policy · Google privacy policy

How to ask about your details

Email hello@withszara.com to ask about your enquiry information, request a correction or request deletion. You can also contact Harry directly by email instead of using the form.
