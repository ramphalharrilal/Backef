Good to know

Before
you ask.

Is monthly maintenance required?

No. For new customers, website care is optional. We quote hosting and any recurring third-party costs separately and explain your options before you sign.

What's included in the $999 website?

A responsive single page with up to five agreed sections, an enquiry form, basic on-page SEO, domain connection and two consolidated revision rounds. Additional pages are available in the Business package.

How long does a website take?

We agree the timeline in your proposal after reviewing the content and requirements. Your feedback and any additional scope can affect delivery.

What if I already have a website?

Send us the link. We'll assess whether a focused improvement, redesign or maintenance plan makes the most sense.

Can you guarantee Google rankings or sales?

No. Basic SEO helps search engines understand the site but does not guarantee rankings, traffic, leads or sales.

Do you work across the US?

Yes. We work remotely with businesses throughout the United States.
