Tell us about your project ↗

Start with what visitors need

A service company needs to explain what it does and who it serves. An organization needs people to find its information. A nonprofit needs its mission and programs to make sense to someone arriving for the first time. We start by agreeing what a visitor should understand and do, then build the page structure around that.

Bring us your current website, a rough brief or a problem you cannot quite describe. We will identify the pages, content and features needed for the agreed project. A larger website receives its own scope and quote rather than being forced into a five page package.

What the build covers

  • A page structure and responsive design for phones, tablets and desktop screens.
  • Clear service or organizational information and practical calls to action.
  • An enquiry form, with delivery tested before launch.
  • Basic on page SEO, domain connection and arrangement of supplied content.
  • Review points and a written scope so both sides know what is included.

Copywriting, ecommerce, booking systems, custom integrations and branding require separate scope. We will identify third party subscriptions and other costs before you approve them.

Working together remotely

Harry is your project contact. We use written scope and consolidated feedback to keep decisions clear. For US clients, quotes are in USD and meeting or review times are agreed around your US time zone. Remote delivery also supports Caribbean and international clients; it does not require a visit to an office.

Tell us who approves the work, when your content will be ready and any date the project must support. Those details help us propose a realistic schedule.

From first conversation to launch

  1. Tell us what you need. Share the business, audience and main action you want visitors to take.
  2. Receive a scope and quote. We agree pages, features, responsibilities and price.
  3. Design and build. We arrange your supplied content around the agreed direction.
  4. Review and launch. You provide consolidated feedback; we check the agreed pages, links and enquiry delivery.
  5. Keep it useful. Agree ongoing maintenance and small updates where needed.

A defined package or a custom quote

That price applies to the defined package, not every SZARA project. Broader websites and additional features are quoted separately. The package targets 3 to 4 weeks once the required content and access are ready; review or scope changes can affect the schedule.
