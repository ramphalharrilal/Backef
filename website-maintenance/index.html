Website maintenance

Keep your website
working for you.

Opening hours change. Services evolve. Someone needs to look after the website after launch. SZARA Care gives the website we build a defined support arrangement and a clear contact.

Tell us about your project ↗

Support with a clear boundary

SZARA Care covers hosting, routine maintenance, website checks and up to one hour of small edits each month for the website included in the agreed build. Typical edits include replacement text or photographs, opening hours and contact details. Unused editing time does not roll over.

The proposal states the platform, check frequency and maintenance tasks. A static website and a site using a third party platform can have different maintenance needs, so those details are agreed for the actual project.

What happens when you need a change

Email hello@withszara.com with the page link, the requested change and any replacement content. We provide an initial reply within two business days, Monday to Friday excluding US federal holidays. The reply confirms the next step; it is not a guarantee that every request will be completed within that time.

If the work exceeds the monthly editing allowance, changes the structure or adds a feature, we quote the additional work for approval first. New pages, redesigns, ecommerce, booking systems and custom integrations are not small edits.

Price and term

Care is US$149/month from launch as part of the US$999 website setup package. The initial agreement is 12 months, then month to month with 30 days’ cancellation notice. During the initial term, cancellation requires 30 days’ notice, Care payments through that period and a single US$149 exit fee; no remaining monthly balance is due. After the initial term there is no exit fee.

Domain renewals, paid email, third party subscriptions and advertising spend are separate. Care does not promise unlimited edits, 24/7 support, guaranteed rankings or guaranteed leads.

Already have a website?

Send us the link and tell us which platform and hosting you use, who has access and what needs attention. We need to review an existing site before agreeing responsibility, scope or a price. The standard Care price should not be taken as an automatic offer to maintain any website on any platform.

Practical support in the portfolio

Harry’s work for Kenswick Meadows Community Association includes website management, resident resources, documents, notices and technical support. It demonstrates the kind of continuing work that helps a website remain useful; it is not a claim that every project uses the same Care package.

A planned handover

Completed website files are available after the build fees are paid, subject to third party licences. Domain and service account access, handover contents and any transfer work are set out in the proposal. Separately agreed transfer work or third party fees are not included automatically.

We work remotely with US and international clients. Agree review times around your time zone and keep the ongoing tasks in writing. If your first priority is a new build, explore website design and development.
