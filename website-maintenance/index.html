Website maintenance

Keep your website

Need someone to update your website after launch? SZARA Care helps with routine checks, small changes and ongoing upkeep. Plans start at $79 per month for websites we build. If you have an existing site, we'll review it before agreeing on support.

Tell us about your project

Website updates without starting over

Maybe your hours have changed, a service page needs updating or customers are having trouble with an enquiry form. You should not need a full redesign for a small change. We offer scheduled website checks and approved updates, depending on your plan.

We support Texas businesses, including the Houston area, remotely and can work with clients elsewhere in the US. Building a new website? Explore the website packages first. For existing websites, send the link so we can check the platform and quote the work.

How requests work

Email hello@withszara.com with the page link, the change you need and any replacement text or images. We'll acknowledge requests according to the plan's agreed response target and confirm timing and scope.

Minor edits include changes to existing text, photographs, opening hours and contact details. New pages, redesigns, ecommerce, booking systems, custom integrations, emergency recovery and general IT support are quoted separately.

Billing and hosting

All prices are in USD. Hosting and domain renewals are quoted separately unless expressly included in your proposal. Paid plugins, subscriptions, email services and advertising spend are not included.

Care is optional for new website builds. Your proposal will explain billing, start date, renewal, cancellation notice, handover and any separately approved charges before you commit. Existing signed customer agreements remain governed by their own terms.

Already have a website?

Send us the link and tell us which platform and hosting you use, who has access and what needs attention. We need to review an existing site before agreeing responsibility, scope or a price. We’ll confirm whether we can support it and send a quote before starting.

Practical support in the portfolio

SZARA’s work for Kenswick Meadows Community Association includes website management, resident resources, documents, notices and technical support. It demonstrates the kind of continuing work that helps a website remain useful; it is not a claim that every project uses the same Care package.

A planned handover

Completed website files are available after the build fees are paid, subject to third party licences. Domain and service account access, handover contents and any transfer work are set out in the proposal. Separately agreed transfer work or third party fees are not included automatically.

We work remotely with US and international clients. Agree review times around your time zone and keep the ongoing tasks in writing. If your first priority is a new build, explore website design and development.
