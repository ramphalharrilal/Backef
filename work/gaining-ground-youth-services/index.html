Gaining Ground case study

Give the mission
a public home.

Website content and storytelling for Gaining Ground Youth Services, a Houston based nonprofit represented in Harry’s portfolio.

Tell us about your project ↗

The client need

Gaining Ground Youth Services presents community programs and services to people seeking information about its work. A first time visitor needs context: the mission, what the organization offers and the people and story behind it.

Dale Ray’s personal story and the organization’s media add depth, but they also need a clear place within the public presentation. The project brought that material together so visitors could explore it.

The contribution

Harry organized the nonprofit’s mission, services, testimonials, videos and Dale Ray’s life story into a public portfolio supporting its community work. The documented contribution includes website presentation, content organization, storytelling, video and brand direction.

  • Mission and service information organized for a public audience.
  • Founder story and approved testimonials presented with context.
  • Video and storytelling material incorporated into the presentation.
  • A connected public view of the organization and its community work.

The purpose of the finished work

The website gives visitors a place to learn about the nonprofit and explore its story and services. The project brings information about the mission, services and founder into one public presentation.
