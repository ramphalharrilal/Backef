Kenswick Meadows case study

Useful information.
A clearer community resource.

Website design, administration and ongoing support for Kenswick Meadows Community Association, serving a community in Humble, Texas.

Tell us about your project ↗︎

The client need

A community association needs a public place for the information residents repeatedly ask for: association documents, forms, notices, contact details and payment information. Those resources need an owner after the initial website is built.

The association’s live site includes community updates and resident resources. SZARA’s contribution covers the website and its ongoing public information.

The work

SZARA designed and built the website and continues to manage its resident resources, forms, payment information, notices, documents and technical support. The contribution combines website design with practical administration of public information.

  • Website design and page organization.
  • Resident resources and document publishing.
  • Forms and payment information.
  • Notices, updates and ongoing technical support.

The solution and purpose

Bringing these materials into a public website gives residents a place to look for association information and a route to contact the organization. Ongoing management supports updates as the information changes.

What this project demonstrates

The work illustrates how a community website can combine a clear information structure with ongoing administration. Boards evaluating a website provider can ask to see how resident forms, documents, payment instructions and notices will be organized and who will maintain them after launch. The case study does not claim a measured increase in traffic or resident satisfaction.
